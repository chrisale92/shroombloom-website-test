// the process

From box to harvest in 6 simple steps

STEP 01

Sanitize & prepare

Start your grow by creating a sterile environment. Wash your hands thoroughly and put on the included gloves, then use the alcohol swab to fully clean the injection port.

Next, prepare your spore or liquid culture syringe by following the instructions from your genetics supplier (culture not included). Once your equipment is ready and the area is clean, you can begin.

STEP 02

Inject your culture

Insert your syringe into the self-healing cultivation port and inject your spores or liquid culture directly into the grain. The port seals itself the moment you withdraw the needle, keeping everything contaminant-free.

Five minutes from delivery to your first grow cycle — no tutorials, no extra tools.

STEP 03

Let it colonize

Set the bag somewhere out of direct sunlight and let the mycelium do the work. The rapid-rooting milo grain and BloomBoost nutrient blend accelerate colonization, with visible white growth in days.

The 0.2-micron filter handles air exchange automatically — no fanning, no opening the bag.

STEP 04

Mix to turbocharge

Once the grain is fully colonized, give the bag a mix to distribute the mycelium evenly through the substrate. This turbocharges the final growth stage and sets up a heavy, even flush.

STEP 05

Fruiting

The bag forms its own self-regulating micro-climate, triggering pins and then full fruiting bodies. No tents or humidifiers needed — the self-contained environment handles humidity and gas exchange for you.

STEP 06

Harvest pounds

When the caps are ready, twist and pick. Each bag is engineered for multiple heavy flushes, so re-hydrate and repeat to keep harvesting pounds, not ounces, from a single kit.
