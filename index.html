The full process

From box to harvest in 6 steps.

Simple enough for a first-time grower, detailed enough to feel guided at every stage.

Step 01

Sanitize & prepare.

Create a clean environment. Put on the included gloves and use the alcohol wipe to clean the injection port fully.

Prepare your spore or liquid culture syringe according to your supplier’s instructions. Once everything is clean, you’re ready.

Step 02

Inject your culture.

Push your syringe into the self-healing injection port and slowly inject. The port seals itself the moment you remove the syringe.

Five minutes from delivery to your first active grow cycle.

Step 03

Let it colonize.

Set the bag out of direct sunlight. The rapid-rooting grain and BloomBoost nutrient blend help support visible white growth.

The pro filter handles air exchange automatically. No fanning. No opening the bag.

Step 04

Break & shake.

Once fully colonized with white mycelium, thoroughly mix the bag to distribute growth through the substrate.

This one step helps create a more even final growth stage.

Step 05

Fruiting begins.

The bag forms its own simplified micro-climate, helping pins develop into full fruiting bodies.

No tent, tub, or humidifier required.

Step 06

Harvest & repeat.

When caps reach full size, harvest at the base and follow the guide for future rounds.

The system is designed for multiple flushes from a single grow bag.
